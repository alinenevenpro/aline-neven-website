We often understand why we feel the way we do long before our body believes we're safe.

You might know you can slow down, set boundaries or let go, yet your nervous system keeps responding as if it has to protect you.

My work brings together conversation, body awareness and movement as part of a trauma-informed somatic therapy approach. The aim is to help create change that is not only understood, but experienced.

MY APPROACH TO TRAUMA-INFORMED SOMATIC THERAPY

Every person arrives with a different story.

There is no fixed protocol, no expectation to revisit painful memories, and no pressure to perform.

Instead, we work collaboratively at a pace that feels manageable, exploring what your body is communicating and what it might need to move forward.

Sessions may include:

  • Conversation
  • Nervous system education
  • Somatic awareness
  • Breathwork
  • Movement
  • Yoga-inspired practices
  • Mindfulness
  • Trauma processing
  • Practical tools to integrate into daily life

Not every session includes all of these. The process is guided by what feels most supportive for you.

INDIVIDUAL SESSIONS

Sessions last 75 minutes, allowing enough space to slow down, explore and integrate.

Rather than rushing towards solutions, we create time to notice patterns, reconnect with your body and build a greater sense of safety and resilience.

BEYOND THE SESSION

The work doesn't stop when the session ends.

Together we'll explore small, realistic practices that help bring what we discover into your everyday life.

The goal isn't to add another task to your week. It's to help you build a different relationship with yourself.

THIS WORK MAY BE FOR YOU IF YOU...

  • feel disconnected from yourself
  • are navigating grief, burnout, anxiety or life transitions
  • find yourself stuck in patterns that no longer serve you
  • want more than insight alone
  • are curious about the connection between body and mind
