PERFECTIONISM

Perfectionism is often misunderstood as simply having high standards. In reality, it is frequently driven by fear. Fear of failure, criticism or not being enough. It can keep the nervous system in a constant state of pressure.

You might notice:

  • Fear of making mistakes
  • Difficulty finishing projects
  • Overthinking decisions
  • Self-criticism
  • Constantly raising the bar
  • Feeling like nothing is ever good enough

Together we'll explore the protective role perfectionism has played in your life and develop new ways of approaching yourself with greater flexibility and self-compassion.
