Starting something new can feel like a big step. You don't have to know exactly what you need before we begin.

There are two ways to start. You can book a free discovery call first, or go directly ahead and book a 75-minute individual somatic counselling session.

01

FREE DISCOVERY CALL

30 MINUTES · FREE

Not sure if we're the right fit? A free online discovery call gives us a chance to meet, talk about what you're looking for and answer any questions you may have.

There's no obligation to book a session afterwards.

02

INDIVIDUAL SESSION

75 MINUTES · €65

A spacious session to slow down, explore what's present and reconnect with your body, emotions and needs.

Sessions are collaborative and may include conversation, somatic practices and movement, depending on what feels supportive for you.

WHAT TO
EXPECT

BEFORE WE MEET

You'll receive a confirmation with the details of your appointment. Take a moment to create some quiet space around the session where possible.

DURING THE SESSION

We'll begin by checking in with what's present for you. From there, we'll explore what feels relevant that day, combining conversation with somatic work where appropriate.

AFTERWARDS

We'll take some time to reflect and integrate before closing the session. There is no expectation to have everything figured out by the time we finish.

Not sure whether this work is right for you? You can always start with the free discovery call.
