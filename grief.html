GRIEF

Grief is a natural response to loss, but it doesn't only affect our emotions. It can influence our nervous system, our relationships and even how we experience our own body. Grief has no fixed timeline and often changes over time.

You might notice:

  • Waves of sadness, anger or guilt
  • Feeling disconnected from yourself or others
  • Difficulty sleeping
  • Tightness in the chest or throat
  • Fatigue or loss of motivation
  • Difficulty concentrating or remembering things

Our work creates space for grief without trying to rush or "fix" it. Together we'll explore how grief is showing up in both your body and your life, supporting you in finding ways to carry your loss while reconnecting with yourself.
