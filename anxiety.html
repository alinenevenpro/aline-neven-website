ANXIETY

Anxiety is the body's way of preparing us for potential danger. While this response is essential for survival, it can become overactive, leaving the nervous system in a constant state of alertness even when no immediate threat is present.

You might notice:

  • Racing thoughts
  • Difficulty relaxing
  • Constant worrying
  • Restlessness
  • Shallow breathing
  • Increased heart rate
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Feeling on edge

Rather than trying to eliminate anxiety, we'll work towards understanding how your nervous system responds to stress and develop greater flexibility, helping your body recognise when it is safe to settle again.
