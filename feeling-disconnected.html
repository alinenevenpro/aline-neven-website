FEELING DISCONNECTED

Sometimes there isn't a single event or diagnosis. You simply know that you don't quite feel like yourself anymore. You may feel distant from your body, your emotions or the things that once brought you joy.

You might notice:

  • Feeling numb or emotionally flat
  • Moving through life on autopilot
  • Loss of motivation
  • Difficulty identifying your emotions
  • Feeling disconnected from your body
  • A sense that something is missing

Together we'll gently explore this disconnection, creating space to reconnect with yourself through greater awareness, safety and embodied experience.
