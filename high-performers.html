WHO I WORK WITH / 08

HIGH PERFORMERS

Many people who appear successful on the outside quietly struggle with constant pressure on the inside. High achievers often learn to push through discomfort, ignore their body's signals and tie their self-worth to productivity.

You might notice:

  • Difficulty slowing down
  • Feeling guilty when resting
  • Constantly chasing the next goal
  • Fear of failure
  • Perfectionistic thinking
  • Feeling exhausted despite achieving more

Together we'll explore what drives these patterns, helping you build a healthier relationship with ambition while creating more balance, flexibility and self-compassion.
